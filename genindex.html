Questions

Common questions.

Do you offer day-to-day IT support or network setup?

No. We focus on projects: websites, automation, digital forms, Microsoft 365 solutions, software decisions, and planning. If you need a help desk or network work, we're happy to point you to someone and work alongside them.

Do we need to buy new software?

Often not. Many businesses already pay for Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace and use a fraction of it. We start with what you have and only recommend something new when it clearly pays off.

We're only a few people. Is that too small?

No. Small teams feel paperwork and manual work the most, and a single well-built form or automation can save hours every week.

What happens after the project is done?

You get training and written documentation, so your team can run it on its own. If you want changes later, we scope them as small follow-on projects.
