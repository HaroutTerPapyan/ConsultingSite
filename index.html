The connective tissue: interfaces

KP Learn and IDM aren't just applications — they're endpoints on a network of HR data moving between systems. The interface and integration work underneath them is what actually keeps HR applications usable, secure, and in sync.

  • Data integration interfaces between HR applications, built with ETL processes
  • API and ETL solutions that automate identity provisioning
  • Reduced manual access-management workload 40%+ through that automation
  • IAM design across HR Suite applications
  • Solutions architecture spanning the full HR and Learning technology organization
Who this is for

HR and People Ops leaders running (or replacing) an LMS or a disability/accommodation system, and IT teams who need the interfaces between HR applications — and the identity provisioning that rides on them — designed by someone who has owned both the systems and the integrations.
