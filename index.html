Flagship Specialty

HR applications and the interfaces that connect them.

Enterprise HR systems — core HR/HCM, learning management, and disability management & accommodation requests — owned end to end, plus the integration work that ties HR applications together and keeps identity and access flowing between them. This is the core of the practice.

Discuss an engagement

Three disciplines, owned end to end

Discipline

HCM & Core HR Applications

  • HCM Cloud strategy, architecture, and migration planning
  • HR Suite application architecture and modernization
  • Legacy-to-cloud migration technical leadership
  • SaaS ecosystem integration across HR platforms
Discipline

Learning Management (LMS) & Compliance Training

  • LMS strategy and product roadmap ownership — 300,000+ users
  • Vendor selection, contracting, and ongoing governance — $1M+ managed
  • Compliance training program design and rollout
  • Platform adoption, UX improvement, and support-volume reduction
Discipline

Disability Management & Accommodation Requests

  • Enterprise disability case management system strategy
  • Employee and candidate workplace accommodation request portals
  • ADA-related workflow design, built with legal review in mind
  • Injury reporting and OSHA recordkeeping workflows
  • Case document and image storage migration into a unified system

Proof from the field

The product roadmap for a 300,000+ user learning & compliance platform was owned end to end, alongside $1M+ in vendor service contracts. On the disability side, an enterprise case management system was rebuilt from the architecture up through the accommodation portal itself — redesigned “with legal considerations in mind” and later carried through a migration to HCM Cloud, with case document and image storage moved into the unified application along the way.

Underneath both systems, API and ETL work automated identity provisioning and cut manual access-management workload 40%+ — nearly seven years of continuous ownership across core HR, learning, and disability management systems.

The connective tissue: interfaces

HR applications aren't standalone — they're endpoints on a network of HR data moving between systems. The interface and integration work underneath them is what actually keeps HR applications usable, secure, and in sync.

  • Data integration interfaces between HR applications, built with ETL processes
  • API and ETL solutions that automate identity provisioning
  • Reduced manual access-management workload 40%+ through that automation
  • IAM design across HR Suite applications
  • Solutions architecture spanning the full HR and Learning technology organization
Who this is for

HR and People Ops leaders running (or replacing) an HCM platform, an LMS, or a disability/accommodation system, and IT teams who need the interfaces between HR applications — and the identity provisioning that rides on them — designed by someone who has owned both the systems and the integrations.
